[Adblock Plus 2.0]
! Title: Bopbox extras
! Description: Small hand-curated additions for m.youtube.com and open.spotify.com.
! Policy: be conservative. Never block playback-critical endpoints:
!   - *.googlevideo.com/videoplayback, m.youtube.com/youtubei/v1/player|next|browse, i.ytimg.com
!   - open.spotify.com documents, spclient.wg.spotify.com track/playback/metadata endpoints,
!     audio CDNs (audio*-ak-spotify-com.akamaized.net, audio-fa.scdn.co, *.scdn.co)
! A broken player is worse than an ad. Each rule below is commented with its purpose.
!
! ======================== Google ad serving (used by YouTube and Spotify web) ========================
! DoubleClick ad requests / ID sync from YouTube and display ads.
||googleads.g.doubleclick.net^
! AdSense / ad library host (adsbygoogle.js, pagead/js/...).
||pagead2.googlesyndication.com^
! Google Publisher Tag ad servers (Spotify web display ads use GPT).
||pubads.g.doubleclick.net^
||securepubads.g.doubleclick.net^
! Ad conversion / view-through tracking.
||ad.doubleclick.net^
||stats.g.doubleclick.net^
!
! ======================== YouTube (m.youtube.com / www.youtube.com) ========================
! Ad impression reporting endpoint (no effect on content playback).
||youtube.com/api/stats/ads
! Legacy ad-serving and ad-tracking paths.
||youtube.com/pagead/
||youtube.com/ptracking
||youtube.com/get_midroll_
||youtube.com/pcs/activeview
||youtube.com/ad_data_204
||youtube.com/youtubei/v1/player/ad_break
! Ad-specific "doubleclick-style" conversion pings served from youtube.com.
||youtube.com/pagead/conversion/
!
! ======================== Spotify (open.spotify.com) ========================
! Ad decisioning / ad metadata on the spclient API host (track & playback paths are untouched).
! `$important`: uBO unbreak allows all spclient XHR on spotify.com; these two must still be blocked.
||spclient.wg.spotify.com/ads/$important
||spclient.wg.spotify.com/ad-logic/$important
! Desktop-client update checks; never needed by the web player.
||spclient.wg.spotify.com/desktop-update/
! Ad event / impression tracker.
||adeventtracker.spotify.com^
! Marketing analytics and tracking pixels (not used for playback).
||analytics.spotify.com^
||pixel.spotify.com^
||pixel-static.spotify.com^
!
! ======================== Cosmetic: YouTube mobile web ========================
! Promoted video cards in feed and search.
m.youtube.com##ytm-promoted-sparkles-web-renderer
m.youtube.com##ytm-promoted-sparkles-text-search-renderer
m.youtube.com##ytm-promoted-video-renderer
! Generic ad slot container (home feed, watch-next).
m.youtube.com##ad-slot-renderer
m.youtube.com##ytm-rich-item-renderer:has(> ad-slot-renderer)
! Companion (side/below player) ads.
m.youtube.com##ytm-companion-ad-renderer
m.youtube.com##ytm-companion-slot
! "Try YouTube Premium" style bottom promo bar.
m.youtube.com##ytm-mealbar-promo-renderer
! Banner overlay ads drawn on top of the video (not the player itself).
m.youtube.com##.ytp-ad-overlay-container
!
! ======================== Cosmetic: Spotify web ========================
! Upsell / premium banners and ad cards. Names may drift; rules are harmless if absent.
open.spotify.com##[data-testid="upsell-banner"]
open.spotify.com##[data-testid="ad-companion-card"]
open.spotify.com##[data-testid="hpto-banner"]
open.spotify.com##[data-testid="embedded-ad"]
!
! ======================== Scriptlets (indexed only, NOT executed by Bopbox yet) ========================
! uBO-style JSON pruning of ad placements in YouTube player responses.
youtube.com##+js(json-prune, playerResponse.adPlacements playerResponse.playerAds playerResponse.adSlots adPlacements playerAds adSlots)
