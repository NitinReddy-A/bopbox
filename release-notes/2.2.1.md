# Tube v2.2.1: miniplayer and Shorts fixes

**Tube 2.2.1**: fixes for the miniplayer and Shorts that came out of a full review of 2.2.0.

**Fixed**
- **Back in the miniplayer.** After the video moved on by itself (autoplay) or you picked one from the page behind, back could jump the player to the previous video. It now steps back through the page behind the miniplayer, and leaves the app once there is nothing left to go back to.
- **The page behind the miniplayer follows your settings and sleeps in the background.** Changes such as Hide Shorts now reach it straight away, and it no longer keeps working while Tube is in the background.
- **Dislike counts on Shorts** appear on the Short you are watching, not the one above or below it.
- **Tapping a Short with TalkBack or a mouse** pauses and plays it once, instead of pausing and immediately resuming.
- **Dragging the player down** while YouTube swaps the player (an ad ending, a quality change) no longer leaves later miniplayer animations jumpy.
- **Videos start at your quality on Wi-Fi.** The slow-connection cap read Android's bandwidth estimate before anything had been measured, which could hold fast Wi-Fi at 720p. On Wi-Fi only Save Data or a 2G/3G link lowers the start; mobile data still starts lower when the link is slow.
- **A link shared to Tube opens once.** Changing the system font size or language, or reopening Tube from Recents after Android closed it, no longer jumps back to that video.
- **Refresh filters always finishes,** even when a list cannot be saved (storage full), instead of spinning until Tube restarts.

**Verified:** 108 JS and 142 Kotlin unit tests, including a test for each fix that fails on 2.2.0. Not yet run against YouTube itself or on a phone.
